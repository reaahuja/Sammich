03 / Simulation & optimization↗

Trailer hitch FEA & optimization

In this course project, I used ANSYS APDL to change a trailer hitch’s geometry and compare the stress and volume of each design.

22% lower stress

ANSYS APDL · FEA · Optimization

Project details +

Queen’s University · MECH 465

Approach

I built the geometry in APDL, set up the loads and boundary conditions, and adjusted about 25 design variables within the project’s constraints.

Outcome

The final model had 22% lower maximum stress and 5% less volume. Those figures came from the FEA study.
